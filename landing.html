Need To Know

FREQUENTLY ASKED

How do I join MC Kingdom?

Install Minecraft 1.21.1 and the required Cobblemon modpack, then connect to play.mc-kingdom.net.

What Minecraft version do I need?

MC Kingdom currently runs Minecraft 1.21.1 with Cobblemon.

Is MC Kingdom free to play?

Yes. You can join and play without purchasing anything.

Can I play with friends?

Absolutely. Build together, explore, trade Pokémon and create your kingdom.

Where can I get help?

Join the MC Kingdom Discord community for support and announcements.
